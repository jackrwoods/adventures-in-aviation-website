# Adventures in Aviation Website — Research Questions

Bring back **concrete examples** rather than abstract descriptions. A screenshot of a color palette, a link to a competitor's site, or a sample blog post for your target audience is worth more than a paragraph of description.

---

## 1. Audience & Content Strategy

- What are the target age ranges? (5–8, 8–12, teens, or all of the above?)
- What content types will you publish? (Blog posts, videos, interactive quizzes, printable activities, curriculum guides?)
- Who writes the content and how often is it published?
- What is the audience's baseline aviation knowledge? (Complete beginners, or kids already in programs like Young Eagles?)

---

## 2. Visual Identity & Brand

- Do you have an existing logo or brand assets, or are we designing from scratch?
- What imagery sources will you use? (Stock photos, original photography, illustrations, AI-generated visuals?)
- What specifically feels dated or wrong in the current/reference design? (Too corporate, too busy, wrong age targeting?)

---

## 3. Technical Requirements

- Do you have a preferred static site generator, or are you open to recommendations?
- Where will the site be hosted? (Netlify, Vercel, GitHub Pages, self-hosted?)
- Will non-technical staff need to publish content? If so, what CMS or markdown workflow should they use?
- Is this a new domain or an existing domain to migrate?

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

- Does the site need to accept donations or process payments?
- Will there be an email newsletter? If so, which provider? (Mailchimp, ConvertKit, etc.)
- Do children need site search, or is curated navigation sufficient?
- What accessibility standard is required? (WCAG 2.1 AA is baseline; is AAA needed?)
- What COPPA/privacy policy and consent flows are required for a children's audience?

---

## 5. Navigation & Information Architecture

- How should content be organized? (By vehicle type, career path, age group, STEM subject?)
- Will there be related content cross-linking or sequenced lesson plans?
- Are there external partner resources to link? (EAA, AOPA, FAA, etc.)

---

## 6. Competitive & Comparative Research

- **3–5 websites you admire** (not necessarily aviation — any educational children's site with UX you like)
- **3–5 things you hate** (sites that feel patronizing, cluttered, or inaccessible to children)

---

## Next Steps

Answer whichever categories you already have information for and bring examples. We will iterate from there.
